### 6\. PriceRunner (Klarna Group)

* **Primary Regions**: UK, Sweden, Denmark  
* **Focus / Niche**: General retail, electronics, clothing, home goods

### 7\. Keepa

* **Primary Regions**: Global (Amazon Markets)  
* **Focus / Niche**: Amazon ecosystem tracking  
* **Standout Features**: Provides scientific-grade, highly interactive charts tracking historical price changes, stock availability, and sales ranks.  
* **Publisher Monetization**: Operates on a premium API subscription model for developers alongside standard Amazon Associates (CPA) affiliate outlinks.

### 8\. CamelCamelCamel

* **Primary Regions**: US, UK, EU, Canada, Australia  
* **Focus / Niche**: Amazon ecosystem tracking  
* **Standout Features**: A widely popular, consumer-focused Amazon price tracker providing simple historical line charts, browser extensions, and email price drop notifications.  
* **Publisher Monetization**: Relies entirely on CPA affiliate monetization through the Amazon Associates Program.

### 9\. Compare Club

* **Primary Regions**: Australia  
* **Focus / Niche**: High-consideration services (health insurance, energy bills, life insurance, finance)

* **Publisher Monetization**: Driven by high-ticket direct lead-generation bounties rather than standard retail product clicks.

### 10\. Skinflint (Geizhals Network)

* **Primary Regions**: UK & Europe  
* **Focus / Niche**: Computer hardware, consumer tech, office equipment  
* **Standout Features**: Renowned for exceptionally detailed product attribute mapping and filter options, making it a favorite tool for dropshippers and PC builders.  
* **Publisher Monetization**: Monetized through a B2B premium merchant listing model and partner CPC product feeds.
